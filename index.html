Methodology

Every input is dated and sourced, and the same inputs always give the same result.

  1. Score three factors from 0 to 100%

    FactorWeightHow it is scored
    Progress50%The furthest stage reached:
    • 10Talks launched
    • 20Scope agreed
    • 30Negotiating rounds
    • 60Framework agreed
    • 75Text almost final
    • 85Text in legal review
    • 95Signing date fixed
    • 100Signed
    Obstacles30%100% minus each live obstacle, restored when it is resolved:
    • −40Talks suspended
    • −25Punitive measure, dated threat or legal basis struck down
    • −15Core issue or pending review
    • −5Side irritant
    Intent20%Recent tone of what both sides say and do:
    • ±2Statements, at face value
    • +1Meetings
    • −1Postponed rounds
    • −2Missed signing dates

    Each event’s weight halves every 30 days, so silence drifts back to neutral.

  2. Combine them and set the clock

    Like the Doomsday Clock, the dial shows the last hour: 0% is 60 minutes out, signing is midnight.

    Estimate = 50% × Progress + 30% × Obstacles + 20% × Intent
    Minutes to midnight = 60 × (100% − Estimate)

  3. Convert to a chance of signing

    An S-curve centred on 70% gives the 90-day chance. The 30- and 60-day figures spread it evenly across the three months.

    Estimate → 90-day chance:50% → 3%60% → 16%70% → 50%80% → 84%90% → 97%

  4. Update daily

    New inputs are added every day. Only public information counts.
