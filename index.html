      How the estimate is built

      Three factors, each scored 0 to 100%, combined by weight into one estimate. The estimate sets the time on the clock.

      Methodology

      Every input is dated and sourced, and the same inputs always give the same result.

      1. Score three factors from 0 to 100%

        FactorWeightHow it is scored

      2. Combine them and set the clock

        Like the Doomsday Clock, the dial shows the last hour: 0% is 60 minutes out, signing is midnight.

        Estimate = 50% × Progress + 30% × Obstacles + 20% × Intent
        Minutes to midnight = 60 × (100% − Estimate)

      3. Convert to a chance of signing

        An S-curve centred on 70% gives the 90-day chance. The 30- and 60-day figures spread it evenly across the three months.

      4. Update weekly

        New inputs are added every week. Only public information counts.
